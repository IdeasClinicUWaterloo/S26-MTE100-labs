## Part 1: Arduino Introduction
This module serves as an introduction to microcontrollers. You will learn how to perform a variety of tasks, including configuring digital and analog pins in the Arduino IDE, reading data from the Serial Monitor, and becoming familiar with basic electronic components such as LEDs, resistors, and potentiometers.

## Part 2: Pong!
In this module, you will begin creating your own Pong game! The game will use a small OLED display and at least two push buttons. You will apply the skills learned in the first module to build the foundation of the game.
